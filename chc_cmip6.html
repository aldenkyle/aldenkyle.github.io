Web Development|Analysis|Design: Explore Potential Climate Extremes from the Climate Hazards Center

In 2024 the Climate Hazards Center published "High resolution climate change observations and projections for the evaluation of heat-related extremes" which included a series of tens of thousands of global girdded daily and/or monthly extreme weather estimates and projections. I further transformed these data-via an automated python script-to get the average, minimum, and maximum number of days an area may experience an extreme within each second-order administrative unit (via fieldmaps.io) in developing countries around the world.

Source: Williams, E., Funk, C., Peterson, P. et al. High resolution climate change observations and projections for the evaluation of heat-related extremes. Sci Data 11, 261 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03074-w
